What a cooling service can involve

A contractor may start by asking about the symptoms, such as when they began and whether they happen consistently. An assessment can help identify whether the next step is maintenance, a repair, or a broader equipment project. The contractor should explain the findings and the work they recommend, so you can decide how to proceed.

For a repair, ask what part or condition needs attention and whether the work addresses the cause or only a symptom. For routine upkeep, ask what tasks are included and whether the contractor found anything that needs separate attention. For installation planning, ask what information is needed about the space and existing equipment before discussing options.

A few terms you may hear:
- Diagnosis: The process of checking the system to identify a problem.
- Maintenance: Routine work intended to check or care for equipment.
- Installation: Setting up new equipment or replacing existing equipment.

The time needed varies by job. A simple assessment is different from a repair that needs parts or a larger installation project, so ask the contractor for a timing estimate after they understand the work.
