What a heating visit may cover

The contractor may begin by asking what the equipment is doing, when the concern started, and whether it has changed over time. An assessment helps determine whether the issue calls for routine maintenance, a repair, or a discussion about installation or replacement. The provider should explain the findings in everyday language and tell you what work they recommend.

If a repair is suggested, ask what condition it addresses and whether further checks are needed. For maintenance, ask what tasks are included and whether the visit is limited to routine care. If you’re considering an installation, ask what details the contractor needs about the existing equipment and the space before discussing possible approaches.

Here are a few terms you may hear:
- Assessment: A review of the equipment and reported concern.
- Maintenance: Routine care or checks for a heating system.
- Installation: Setting up new equipment or replacing existing equipment.

Timing depends on the work involved. A straightforward check may differ from a repair needing parts or a larger installation project. Ask for a timing estimate after the contractor understands the job.
